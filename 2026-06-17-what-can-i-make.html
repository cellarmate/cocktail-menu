Substitution Candidates

Drink Status Substitution Tradeoff
French 75 variant Plymouth gin -> Harahorn gin; cane sugar syrup -> simple syrup. Still very makeable. Harahorn changes the gin profile; simple syrup is cleaner and less cane-rich.
Daiquiri close Cane sugar syrup -> simple syrup. Cleaner and a little less deep, but structurally sound.
Live Free Or Die variant Plymouth gin -> Harahorn gin. Sparkling gin sour with Cointreau and maraschino. Likely tasty, but sweeter than the top picks.
La Dolce Vita variant Rye whiskey -> Barrell Foundation bourbon. Rounder, sweeter, and less spicy. Use the higher-proof bourbon to stand up to Campari and St-Germain.
Paper Plane-ish creative Amaro Nonino -> Amaro Montenegro; Aperol -> Select Aperitivo. Not book-exact, but bourbon, lemon, Montenegro, and Select should make a balanced bittersweet sour.

Best Next Prep Or Purchase

  • Buy Angostura bitters. It unlocks or improves Fitzgerald, Mojito, Whiskey Sour garnish, and many whiskey or rum templates. Orange bitters are not a direct substitute.
  • Buy sweet vermouth. Biggest stirred-drink unlock with your bourbon, Campari, rum, mezcal, and orange bitters.
  • Buy grapefruit or grapefruit juice. Brown Derby becomes exact, and grapefruit twists improve Elder Fashion Royale.
  • Make demerara or cane syrup. Cheap prep that converts several close matches into stronger exact or near-exact builds.
  • Buy mint if you want Juleps or Mojitos. Mint Julep is database-exact because mint is treated as garnish, but practically it is not worth making without mint.
