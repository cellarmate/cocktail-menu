Division Bell-Style Select Mezcal

variant Glass: coupe Method: shaken

A mezcal, lime, maraschino, and bitter aperitivo sour.

Substitution: Aperol -> Select Aperitivo. Select is more bitter and herbal; this already worked at 9/10.
AmountIngredientNotes
1 1/2 ozMezcalUse 400 Conejos.
3/4 ozSelect AperitivoSub for Aperol.
1/2 ozLuxardo maraschinoKeep this amount for balance.
3/4 ozLime juiceFresh squeezed.

Instructions: Shake with ice and strain into a coupe.

Best Next Prep Or Purchase

  • Buy Angostura bitters. It improves Whiskey Sour garnish and unlocks Fitzgerald, Oaxaca Old-Fashioned, Mojito, and many whiskey/rum templates.
  • Buy sweet vermouth. Biggest stirred-drink unlock: Boulevardier, Negroni variants, and several whiskey/agave stirred drinks.
  • Buy grapefruit. Brown Derby becomes exact, and grapefruit twists improve Elder Fashion Royale and Spaghetti Western.
  • Make cinnamon bark syrup and demerara/cane syrup. Cheap prep that adds Grouse Rampant, Buffalo Soldier, and better daiquiri variants.
  • Buy pisco if you want to use the new egg white. Pisco Sour is now one bottle away.
